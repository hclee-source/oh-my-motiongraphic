# Golden Rabbit — Soft Studio

Calm and crafted rather than loud. Rhythm comes from objects moving in depth: a book turns to show
its spine, a cover swings open, a chat bubble types. It doesn't come from full-screen color slams. Each
scene owns one hero device and one cover-derived pastel. Scenes hand off with a shape transition (iris
from the book, blinds, a blob) generated by `make_transitions.py`, so hard cuts are never seen.

## Do / Don't
- Do put the light/bold contrast inside Korean lines (300 vs 700) and let objects overlap type (type behind the book).
- Do hold at least once for ≥1.5s on a beautiful still (an open book with only the grain moving).
- Don't use label boxes, confetti, marquees, HUD chrome, hard offset shadows or yellow/black register flips. Those belong to bold-beat.
- Don't redraw or recreate characters or logos from any supplied key visual; place the files only.
